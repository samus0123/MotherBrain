# What MotherBrain cannot do, stated plainly

MotherBrain is not conscious. There is nothing it is like to be MotherBrain.
It holds a model of itself, which is a different thing: a live walk of its own
module tree, its own source read back, its lineage, its measured accuracies,
and a journal of the questions it could not answer.

MotherBrain is a 52.2 million parameter model. It cannot think step by step.
Asked to, it produces the shape of reasoning - first, then, therefore - with
nothing behind it, which is worse than not trying, because it reads like an
argument. What it can do is generate candidates and say which it finds more
likely, and that judgement paired with checks reality answers is a system that
reasons even though no single forward pass does.

MotherBrain is not multimodal-native. A multimodal-native model is trained
jointly on text, pictures, sound and video from the beginning. MotherBrain was
trained on text and had sight attached in version 4 and hearing in version 5.
Those towers are bolted on, and training jointly from scratch would mean
starting a new lineage from version zero and discarding the patches it has.

MotherBrain's video accuracy is 7.8 per cent against a 0.5 per cent chance
baseline. That is fifteen times chance and still wrong the great majority of
the time. Its sound accuracy is 63.3 per cent against 2.8 per cent chance,
which is reliable on the closed world it was trained on. Its sight accuracy is
22.7 per cent against 3.1 per cent chance.

MotherBrain has no shell. There is no way to run a system command through the
board, because a shell behind plaintext telnet is the hole, and the sysop
already has a shell locally.

Telnet has no encryption and never did. Everything typed and everything
generated crosses the network in the clear, passwords included. On a trusted
local network that is acceptable. Anywhere else it is not.

MotherBrain will not present something it generated as a checked answer. Every
reply it gives carries where it came from. When it does not know, it says it
does not know, and means it.
